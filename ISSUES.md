- (8.27) ENTAILMENT STATED BACK (engine build, 2026-08-27, standing unless Don objects): the projection algebra needed one rule the boolean ruling did not state, derived from "cross-frame projection only through staples": a PLACEMENT locates a fact and does not carry the object's other facts — an object on two calendars does not relate those calendars, so its calendar-A fact never draws on calendar B; reached through a STAPLE it is the opposite claim (the author connected them, "so we can project the two relative to each other"), so a stapled far object's placements DO count, one hop out. REFINED BY DON (2026-08-27), the governing vocabulary — CONNECTION IS NOT INCLUSION: "an event on two frames does connect them but does not include them. This is the Lines lens in action: it shows the shape of the flow of time across multiple [frames] based on where they attach."
