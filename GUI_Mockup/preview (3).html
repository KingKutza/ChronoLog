Mouse wheel zooms smoothly. Drag pans. Units fade in and out as time compresses.
The Prime Line becomes a band once flat, giving room for ticks, blocks, and event marks.
smooth zoom prototype - HTML/SVG only

Shape rule

The Prime Line enters from the top-left, makes a tight bend into the main band, expands vertically while flat, then closes back down and exits at the bottom-right.

Secondary timelines only appear where they overlap the Prime Line. They add a highlight edge and local annotations rather than becoming separate horizontal lanes.

Zoom rule

As the span changes, tick units fade between hours, days, weeks, months, quarters, years, and decades. The data stays the same; the scale changes.

The buttons are only presets. Wheel and slider give the continuous behavior.
