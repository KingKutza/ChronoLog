Geometry

The Prime Line enters on the left edge, bends tightly, expands into a readable band, then pinches down and exits. The band is the work surface.

Secondary lines are continuous paths: descend, ride the top edge while active, then leave. No stacked calendar lanes.

Event rendering

At month scale, sub-day events become symbols inside daily slices. Vertical position is time of day. Labels are withheld unless the item is large enough.

At closer scales, events become cards occupying time width with text inside the card.
